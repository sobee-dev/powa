Who Are We?

POWA is a trusted virtual tech academy and digital agency empowering Africans with in-demand tech and digital skills. Our programs help individuals transition into tech and upgrade their skills. Through project based training and access to thousands of remote job opportunities, we help Africans land top tech roles, build careers, and turn their skills into income.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to close Africa’s skills gap by giving individuals access to affordable tech and digital skills training. We also help businesses grow online and reach more customers through our digital services.

👁️ Our Vision

We envision a future where every young person can access tech education from anywhere in the world and turn their skills into income. Our goal is to connect individuals to mentorship, meaningful earning opportunities, and a supportive community that fosters career, personal, and economic growth.
